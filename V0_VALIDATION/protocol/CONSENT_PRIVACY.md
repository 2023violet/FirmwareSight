# Consent & Privacy

## Default collection

Collect only:
- anonymized Participant ID;
- role/experience/toolchain/ecosystem;
- observed task behavior;
- quotes that do not disclose company secrets;
- purchase/pilot signals.

## Do not collect by default

- real firmware binaries;
- MAP files;
- source code;
- private repositories;
- credentials;
- customer/device identifiers;
- confidential release information.

## Recording

Audio/screen/video recording requires explicit participant consent before recording starts.

If recording is not authorized, use moderated notes only.

## Reporting

Participant identifiers are anonymized.

Quotes must be redacted if they could reveal confidential employer/project information.
