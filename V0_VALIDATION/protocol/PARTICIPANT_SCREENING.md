# Participant Screening

## Minimum eligibility

Participant should have real involvement in embedded firmware development/release.

Record:
- role;
- years of embedded experience;
- primary toolchain;
- MCU/RTOS ecosystem;
- team size;
- release frequency;
- current pre-release checks.

## Preferred cohort

Prioritize:
- GCC / Clang / GNU ld users;
- engineers/leads who ship embedded firmware.

Keil/IAR participants are valuable discovery participants but must not be told that those toolchains are currently Supported.

## Exclude from usability numerator/denominator

- project team members;
- people already briefed on the UI semantics;
- anyone who designed the prototype;
- sessions where the moderator teaches the workflow before tasks.

## Sample rule

Minimum formal external sample: 8  
Target: 12–15

If fewer than 8 eligible sessions are completed, V0 cannot be declared complete.
