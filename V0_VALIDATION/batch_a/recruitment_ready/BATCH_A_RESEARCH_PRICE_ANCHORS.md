# Batch A Research Price Anchors

## Current status

# NOT AUTHORIZED / NOT YET SET

The current baseline and Batch A input do not provide concrete research price anchors.

Therefore this execution does not invent a “reasonable” price.

## Before payment-question sessions

Product Lead may define 2–3 **exploratory** anchors.

For each anchor record:
- currency;
- billing period;
- individual/team context;
- why this anchor is being tested.

These are research stimuli, not product pricing.

## Session record fields

- Price anchor shown
- Participant response
- Self-pay / company-pay
- Approval needed
- Reason
- Objection
- Pilot willingness
