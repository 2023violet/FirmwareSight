# v0.3.0 Audit Resolution

## Conclusion

外部团队发现的问题大部分属实，但性质主要是：
- 文档/治理漏洞
- 领域语义未冻结
- 产品范围口径冲突
- 技术契约未闭环
- 可追溯性不足

不是已经发现可利用的软件安全漏洞，因为 v0.3.0 尚未进入实现。

## Additional issue found during v0.4 review

### Gate state is not just UI
The 4-state/5-state inconsistency changes:
- domain model
- policy semantics
- export schema
- audit history

Therefore it received ADR-0023 rather than a visual-only patch.

## Validation sequence correction

Historical process wanted prototype-first; v0.3 wanted P0-first.

v0.4 adopts:
- V0 prototype workflow validation
- P0 technical slice
in parallel after authorization.

P1 waits for both.
