## Overall verdict

The round is high quality and directionally coherent.

### Adopt as strong conclusions
- visualization should accelerate engineering judgment, not decorate;
- deterministic export/report portability matters;
- integration/History can increase workflow continuity;
- evidence completeness and traceability are stronger expansion themes than arbitrary new screens;
- `.su`, component drift, symbol retention and CI/history are grounded categories;
- all extensions should attach to the existing four verbs;
- current MVP must remain unchanged until validation.

### Correct before baseline adoption
- exploration stage labels were stale relative to v0.4;
- several negative market claims were too absolute;
- some vendor pricing/customer claims were not independently current;
- “Excel/DOCX = fake demand” is stronger than the evidence;
- “cloud cannot do local/self-contained output” is false;
- “no open-source Action precedent” is false as an absolute statement;
- old reports still said “only P0 next”, conflicting with v0.4’s V0+P0 parallel validation.
