- display normal candidate-selection + no-result empty state;

  invite a new Compare. Do not silently reuse another handle.

- no state update from an obsolete async response may clobber a newer pair
  or display result for the wrong selection;
- avoid unhandled promise/re-render errors when leaving/reentering;
- do not introduce uncontrolled background comparison runs.

Recommended solution family: lift the minimal comparison session state to the

controller). The implementation must be justified by the actual code, not
prescribed from habit. Keep the frontend-owned session state small and typed.

DO NOT implement "persistence" via SQLite, backend IPC, localStorage, or new
schema; the comparison diffId is explicitly an ephemeral in-process handle.
Avoid an unsafely hidden-but-mounted huge page if this breaks accessibility or
background IPC; if chosen, demonstrate lifecycle safety and render invariants.
